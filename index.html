How to read this
  • Source: sellerboard automated orders report for the Amazon US seller account, pulled and appended once a day.
  • The sellerboard feed only carries a rolling seven-day window, so days older than that survive only in the history this dashboard accumulates. History begins the day it was first run.
  • A day stays provisional for about 72 hours: orders move Unshipped → Shipped, and some are cancelled or refunded. The most recent day is always partial.
  • Refunds are dated to the original order date, not the refund date, so a recent refund of an old order lands on the old day.
  • Amounts are USD, taken from the order total including tax and shipping charged to the buyer.
